In our first year competing in the Autonomous Platform Cup, our team became the first and only team to drive on track in the competition.

I helped establish the platform from the ground up, with responsibility for developing and integrating its electrical and electronic systems.

Working from initial concept through vehicle integration and competition preparation, I contributed to turning a first-year platform into a functioning autonomous race vehicle.
