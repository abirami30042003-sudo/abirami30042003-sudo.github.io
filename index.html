MSc dissertation · Battery engineering

Formula E Battery and Thermal Modelling

Developed a Formula E-inspired electrochemical-thermal battery model and reconstructed race-drive cycles, vehicle specifications, and battery operating conditions.

Simulated three driving scenarios — fast, average, and slow — and compared a baseline configuration against two cooling architectures: cold-plate and immersion cooling.

Key result: immersion cooling reduced cell temperature from 46.22°C to 31.26°C in the fast-drive scenario at 20% SOC.

MATLAB Simulink Simscape Battery Drive-cycle simulation Cold-plate cooling Immersion cooling
GREEN-LOG · Industry collaboration · Electric powertrain

Autonomous Delivery Vehicle Battery and Powertrain

Developed a 48 V battery and powertrain concept for an autonomous delivery vehicle connected to the GREEN-LOG sustainable logistics project.

Redesigned the battery configuration from 13S21P to 13S50P and evaluated an increase in motor power from 250 W to 600 W.

Key results: 48 V battery system, 13S21P to 13S50P configuration, and 250 W to 600 W motor power.
